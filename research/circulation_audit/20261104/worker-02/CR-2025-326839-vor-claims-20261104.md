**C2 — Human plaque regional pairing/statistical test (plausible REVIEW_CANDIDATE for verification, NOT a proven error).** **Methods > Human Carotid Endarterectomy Plaques** (lines 266–268): specimens are dissected into stable vs vulnerable regions; **Figure 6A/B caption** (lines 379–385): CD68+ tissue-region TRIB3 and Rab27a fluorescence comparisons, n=5; **Figure 6 caption** specifies **Mann–Whitney U** for panels A/B. No assertion of incorrect P, significance, or manipulation, and no assertion that either source cohort was actually paired. Endpoint: regional macrophage fluorescence, not prospective clinical plaque rupture; population: human carotid specimens; comparison stable vs vulnerable tissue; time point revascularization snapshot; estimator unpaired distributional comparison. **Source support SUPPORTED for published methods/figure, model-appropriateness ambiguity only; maturity SOURCE_BACKED; disposition REVIEW_CANDIDATE (verify pairing), not independently adjudicated; provisional S3 reporting/analysis materiality IF matching and inference sensitivity proved; current final severity UNDETERMINED.** Root-cause candidate `plaque-region-pairing-not-specified`.
