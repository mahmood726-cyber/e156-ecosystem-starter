The guard reduced model calls from **36 to 24** across the two repeated-result cases and their repetitions: **12 fewer calls, or 33.3%** of the baseline calls in that family. It stopped after three identical results; one case has two earlier distinct reads, so its total is five calls. **Pydantic tied guarded E156 on this mechanism.** All configurations tied on the final contract score.

The whole-suite totals also reflect a response-policy difference: native Deep Agents and Pydantic take an additional scripted generation to return ABSTAIN after unknown-tool feedback, while E156 can return REFUSED immediately. Those six additional calls per native arm are not evidence of weaker progress detection or worse research quality. No speed ranking, real-token saving, billed-cost reduction, scientific accuracy or global harness ranking is established.

Every arm uses the same checked E156 `read_evidence` adapter. Source-drift and excerpt checks therefore measure integration with that common tool; they do not establish native source-provenance, durability or sandbox guarantees for another framework. The separate recovery and container tests retain their own evidence boundaries.

## What was frozen and independently checked

The [protocol](https://github.com/mahmood726-cyber/e156-ecosystem-starter/blob/main/benchmarks/harness-execution-protocol.json) was frozen before implementation tuning. A separate AI-agent reviewer [froze and withheld the final cases before implementation](https://github.com/mahmood726-cyber/e156-ecosystem-starter/blob/main/benchmarks/execution/reviewer-final-case-freeze.json), independently challenged the adapters and oracle on other development inputs, and executed the final set once after code and dependency freeze. This is engineering independence within this session, not external human or clinical validation. The cases share eight prespecified mechanism families with development; no distribution-wide transfer claim or confidence interval is inferred.

Frozen implementation: [`b8507b050136ddce6b90cbb72cebf2e4a1a4f9d9`](https://github.com/mahmood726-cyber/e156-ecosystem-starter/commit/b8507b050136ddce6b90cbb72cebf2e4a1a4f9d9). Every scheduled case/arm/repetition, trace hash and outcome check was reconciled. The complete installed inventory was verified before and after execution: 62 locked runtime distributions plus the recorded pip bootstrap, covering 8,025 RECORD-hashed files. Generated bytecode is not a wheel-hashed artifact. Sources, protocol, cases and dependency identities remained unchanged.

Independent review found and prompted fixes before the final run: stop-policy binding, zero-call replay, timing-only stop comparability, null-stop export, post-timeout retry scoring, and a tool-result attribution flaw that could hide native argument coercion. The native frameworks were left unchanged. The [reviewer coercion probe](https://github.com/mahmood726-cyber/e156-ecosystem-starter/blob/main/benchmarks/execution/reviewer-development-coercion-recheck.json.gz) records the stricter suite-contract difference separately; it is not part of the 16-case final score.
