# Synthesis findings: evidence snapshot (2 November 2026)

This is a curated, non-exhaustive record of findings from an AI-assisted and human-checked workflow. It is not a validation result for Synthesis v1.3 software. Do not count correspondence as errors, and do not count a planned correction as published.

| Publication | Finding | Status from author/editor correspondence |
|---|---|---|
| STARRT-AKI, NEJM, DOI 10.1056/NEJMoa2000741 | Primary mortality risk-difference CI incorrectly transcribed: intended −3.4 to +3.8 percentage points. | Authors confirmed transcription error and notified journal. |
| EHJ IPD meta-analysis, DOI 10.1093/eurheartj/ehaf220 | Table 1 Q1 treated count/percentage corrected to 1,364 (38.3%). | Authors confirmed; erratum planned. |
| SHARPS, DOI 10.3310/WVVL4786 | Misordered SURE domain summaries; incorrect IQR [85,8] vs [5,8]; PHQ-9 CI corrected to −3.4 to 2.2. | Investigator confirmed three error categories and planned journal contact. |
| Bao et al., DOI 10.1186/s12872-026-06248-z | Incorrect CI reporting and subgroup denominators; additional extraction issues identified during reanalysis. | Author confirmed and initiated correction. |
| Jiang et al., DOI 10.3389/fendo.2025.1742128 | SE imported as SD for KCCQ; wrong treatment label and SUCRA numbers. | Authors acknowledged errors. |
| LZ901 vaccine trial, DOI 10.1038/s41467-025-62800-z | Wrong test description in table footnote; CD4 age ≥70 difference 32.6% should be 19.7%. | Author confirmed. Queried P-values were correct under the actual noninferiority test, so exclude them. |
| Surgical meta-analysis, DOI 10.1007/s11701-026-03713-x | Reversed treatment-arm conversion extraction in multiple primary studies. | Author reported verifying four claims against sources. |
| Zampieri et al., DOI 10.1186/s13054-026-06067-w | Mortality contrast direction and supplemental eFigure e7 vasopressor direction. | Author agreed with both points on 2 November; two distinct endpoints, one reply. |
| APSC M-TEER consensus, DOI 10.15420/japsc.2025.96 | Statement 14 eligibility wording inconsistency. | Journal representative reported first/last author agreement and planned erratum. |

Additional cases needing source-response reconciliation before inclusion in the confirmed numerator: Frontiers finerenone safety NMA, Silverii network meta-analysis, and company disclosure corrections. Do not count BRUIN CLL-322's older PMC manuscript percentage inconsistency as an error in the final Lancet article.

**Evidence rules:** Acknowledgement of receipt is not confirmation. Preserve private author correspondence outside this public repository. Verify the latest published versions and published errata before external reuse. Deduplicate by article, root cause, and affected claim. Real-world sensitivity, precision and false-positive rates remain unknown.
