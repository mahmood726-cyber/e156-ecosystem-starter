## Arithmetic witnesses

The reported interval has geometric midpoint `sqrt(1.18 * 62.52) = 8.58916`, rather than 5.21. Its log half-widths about 5.21 are approximately 1.4851 and 2.4849. Allowing all three printed numbers to vary by 0.005 still cannot restore log symmetry. This is a **conditional diagnostic** for a symmetric log-scale interval; asymmetric methods alone would not prove an error. The independent Results-versus-Figure contradiction does not rely on that assumption. Do not infer an exact corrected CI from the image.

Different screening-stage definitions or omitted boxes could explain parts of the flow, so the question asks the authors for the intended full stage sequence. The final 37-versus-34 contradiction remains visible. A stale figure is a plausible benign production explanation for either root; it would still require reconciliation of the published record. No effect estimate has been refitted.
