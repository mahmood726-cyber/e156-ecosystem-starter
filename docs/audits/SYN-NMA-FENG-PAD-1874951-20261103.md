## Root 1 — total participant denominator (hold)

The [current publisher abstract, Results and Table 1](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/medicine/articles/10.3389/fmed.2026.1874951/full) state **15 RCTs and 1,461 participants** (Abstract Results and §Characteristics around lines 417–418 and 485–488), while Table 1 (15 trial rows, approximately lines 489–505) shows these experimental/control arm sizes, in source order:

```python
EG = [75,78,41,51,115,45,30,34,39,30,90,114,106,30,48]
CG = [75,78,41,51,65,45,30,34,39,30,90,126,106,30,52]
print(len(EG), sum(EG), sum(CG), sum(EG)+sum(CG), sum(EG)+sum(CG)-1461)
# 15 926 892 1818 357
```

This is a reproducible *cross-display difference*: 1,818 rowwise listed arm participants versus 1,461 declared in the narrative. The former is not automatically a unique-participant count, nor proof of incorrectly fitted weights. An unreported outcome-specific sample, exclusions or trial-family identity issue could reconcile the totals. **Status:** SOURCE_BACKED / REPRODUCED_ARITHMETIC; provisional S3; HOLD pending 15 original RCTs and analysis-population mapping.

## Root 2 — SUPER 2022 arm labels (hold)

Table 1's Koelemay et al. (reference 22) row (publisher HTML near line 502) labels **ER+BMT 114 (experimental)** and **SET+BMT 126 (control)**. The [published SUPER trial record](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ejvs.2021.09.042), registry [NCT01385774](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT01385774), and [University of Twente's publication record](https://research.utwente.nl/en/publications/editors-choice-randomised-clinical-trial-of-supervised-exercise-t/) instead report **114 allocated to SET and 126 to ER**, a reversal in the table's displayed allocation. This identifies a different root from the overall denominator.

An incorrect *table label only* is a plausible explanation; no evidence proves that the actual intervention contrast was reversed in the fitted network or that its results change. **Status:** SOURCE_BACKED_CROSS_SOURCE_CONTRADICTION; provisional S3; HOLD pending original contrast input and outcome-specific model reproduction.
