## 1. Adverse-event interval sign: one grouped source disagreement

The publisher's **Abstract Results** says efruxifermin adverse events **0.32 (95% CI +0.06 to +0.70)**. Its **Results §3.4**, discussing Figure 4C, says **0.32 (95% CI −0.06 to +0.70)**. The point estimate and upper bound match; the lower bound differs by 0.12. The first excludes null 0; the second contains zero. The result-scale symmetric-interval midpoint is 0.32 for [−0.06,0.70] versus 0.38 for [+0.06,0.70], **but symmetry is only a conditional diagnostic** and does not establish the original estimator, correct interval, an invalid fitted effect or changed clinical conclusion.

**Provisional severity:** S3 if material outcome wording is confirmed; otherwise S2 local reporting. **Disposition:** FLAG_FOR_CLARIFICATION; count 0 for target until verified estimator identity and independent substantive review.

## 2. Safety ranking prose versus displayed SUCRA: held separately

**Results §3.3** calls placebo the best safety treatment, while reporting placebo SUCRA **69.7%** and mean rank **3.7**, aldafermin **77.3%** and **3.0**, and licogliflozin **72.2%** and **3.5**. Thus higher-is-better SUCRA ranks aldafermin ahead of placebo by 7.6 percentage points, and licogliflozin ahead by 2.5 points. This may be **raw adverse-event rate versus network-relative ranking estimand**, not a numerical model error. SUCRA is not the probability of being best.

**Disposition:** DISCUSSION/HOLD, low-to-moderate materiality unresolved. Original Figure 3C pixels, underlying rank samples, supplement and native network model not independently replayed.
