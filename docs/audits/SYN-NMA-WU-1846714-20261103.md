## Observed contradiction

Section 3.4 (printed PDF p.5) describes every one of the 15 HbA1c comparisons against placebo as statistically significant. The corresponding Figure 2b reports the following mean differences and 95% credible intervals:

| Treatment | MD | 95% CrI | Check |
|---|---:|---|---|
| ITCA650 | −0.80 | −1.8 to +0.21 | Includes zero |
| Visepegenatide | −0.65 | −1.4 to +0.058 | Includes zero |
| Cotadutide | −0.84 | −1.7 to +0.041 | Includes zero |

The plotted intervals therefore do not support that blanket assertion under an interval-exclusion criterion. This is not evidence that the true effects are zero or that these agents are ineffective. The audit cannot determine whether the prose or the plotted intervals require correction.

For FPG, §3.4 describes efficacy for all 12 therapies. Figure 3b (printed p.6) shows visepegenatide MD −0.68 (95% CrI −1.9 to +0.55). Because that prose is less explicit about statistical significance, this is recorded as a related clarification within the same root, not another independently counted error.

## Refutation and scope checks

- Figure 2b was read visually and corroborated in rendered PDF p.5; OCR alone was not used for the interval signs. HTML repeats the claim.
- These are mean differences with a zero null, not ratios with a null of one. Positive upper bounds are too far above zero to be explained by rounding to their displayed precision.
- The section explicitly points to the same figure; a different dose, endpoint or sensitivity analysis was not substituted.
- All 15 HbA1c and 12 FPG rows were transcribed for the bounded interval check. The other 12 HbA1c and 11 FPG intervals exclude zero. These unflagged rows are retained as controls rather than selecting only positives.
- No frequentist p-value is inferred from a Bayesian credible interval. No native model, posterior samples, treatment ranking or primary-trial data were reanalysed.
- Bounded DOI/corrigendum searches retrieved no relevant correction. This is not proof of absence.

## Separate held question: reference-node definition

Eligibility (§2.2) permits other antidiabetic drugs as comparators. Sensitivity analysis (§3.3) describes excluding active-comparator trials to retain placebo-only trials. The primary figures have one node labelled Control, while captions describe placebo comparisons. Without trial-to-node mapping or native inputs this does not establish that reference treatments were incorrectly pooled. It is an unresolved methods question, contributes **zero** to the error count, and must not be described as a proven analytical flaw.
