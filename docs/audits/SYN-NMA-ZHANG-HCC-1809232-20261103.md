## Matched source check

The Introduction (printed PDF p.3, LEAP-012 sentence, reference 17) attributes superiority for both progression-free and overall survival to LEAP-012. The original [randomized trial report](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39798578/) distinguishes the two endpoints:

| LEAP-012 endpoint | HR (95% CI) | One-sided P | Reported significance boundary |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| PFS | 0.66 (0.51–0.84) | 0.0002 | 0.025 |
| OS | 0.80 (0.57–1.11) | 0.087 | 0.0012 |

The original report describes significant PFS improvement and only numerical OS improvement. The [29 October 2025 sponsor update](https://www.merck.com/news/merck-and-eisai-provide-update-on-phase-3-leap-012-trial-in-unresectable-non-metastatic-hepatocellular-carcinoma/) also states that the prespecified OS analysis did not reach statistical significance. The NMA's unqualified OS superiority wording therefore warrants clarification.

The strongest benign explanation is that “superiority” means a favourable numerical direction. That interpretation should not be confused with statistically established superiority. The check uses the cited trial, population, contrast and endpoint; it does not substitute the NMA's indirect comparison or a crude death ratio for the trial HR.

## Negative control and reproducibility

The 35 unique trial-family counts in [source-transcribed JSON](ZHANG-HCC-1809232-inputs.json) sum to the stated 13,595. HIMALAYA (393+389+389), EMERALD-1 (204+207+205) and COSMIC-312 (432+217+188) reconcile to 1,171, 616 and 837. This refutes the proposed aggregate-count duplication lead, while leaving model-level shared-control handling unverified.
