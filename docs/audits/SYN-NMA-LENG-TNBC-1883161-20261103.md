## Claim and source locations

The current article's Abstract Results and Results §§3.1, 3.3.1 and 3.4.1 report **eight RCTs and 2,073 participants**, including the pathological-complete-response (pCR) network. The eight rows of **Results Table 1** print these experimental/control arm sizes:

| RCT | Experimental | Control | Total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| I-SPY2 | 29 | 181 | 210 |
| GeparNuevo | 88 | 86 | 174 |
| NCI-10013 | 45 | 16 | 61 |
| NeoTRIP | 138 | 142 | 280 |
| PLANeT | 78 | 79 | 157 |
| IMpassion031 | 165 | 168 | 333 |
| KEYNOTE-522 | 784 | 390 | 1,174 |
| CamRelief | 222 | 219 | 441 |
| **Total** | **1,549** | **1,281** | **2,830** |

Deterministic check: **1,549 + 1,281 = 2,830**, which differs from 2,073 by **757**. These are **one grouped denominator-accounting root**, not eight errors.
