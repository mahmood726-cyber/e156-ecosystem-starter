**Interpretation:** The examples above were selected for diversity and checkability, **not** drawn randomly. Do not calculate journal error prevalence or expected audit yield from them. A notice's claim that conclusions were unchanged is its reported position; independent reproduction requires source material and an actual analysis. Some official notices amend legacy figures through separate assets instead of replacing the article directly.

## Operational protocol for Nature Portfolio workers 00–08

1. **Already-corrected case protection:** Before assessing a new paper, resolve its original DOI, current publisher Version of Record, notice and DOI links (including Crossmark and Change history), supplementary versions and possible retractions/expression of concern. If the same root issue is already corrected, record **KNOWN_CORRECTED / VERSION_LIMITED**, not a new uncorrected finding or reason to email.
2. **Lane-specific learning:** When the assigned source access and primary DOI review permit, read **up to one** new authoritative author/publisher correction notice from that worker's journal lane per run (not a fixed quota). Record notice DOI, linked original DOI, article/notice dates, error class, exact corrected locus, whether source materials were inspected, impact claimed by the notice, testable prospective check, and possible false positive. If no eligible notice is accessible, record that and focus on the substantive DOI.
3. **Detection order:** Start with **source identity**, sample/group/expt/timepoint/analysis version, then missing/excluded observations, formula and units, denominator/estimand, figure/table/source-data alignment, model and statistical test, and *only then* significance or interpretation. Do not reclassify different metrics, adjusted vs crude estimates, rounded cells, or distinct timepoints as errors.
4. **Anti-error controls:** Use negative controls (NC17 and innocuous typesetting corrections), authentic corrected cases, and genuinely uncorrected papers when available. Confirm the exact original and corrected locations; write source-specific rules, not indiscriminate substring matching. Avoid automated image-duplication accusations without original images and independently checkable context.
5. **Reproduction:** For numeric tests, record exact trusted inputs, formulas, script/command, versions and actual output. If only the notice text is available, mark **NOTICE_ONLY / NOT_REPRODUCED**, not a validated Synthesis catch. Keep uninspected originals and source data visibly uninspected.
6. **Case outcomes:** Distinguish NO_FLAG, RECONCILED/KNOWN_CORRECTED, HOLD/NOT_COMPARABLE, SOURCE_BACKED_CONCERN, reproducible current-version issue, independent human adjudication and verified published amendment. Do not confuse author acknowledgement with published correction.
7. **Human-contact gate:** **Never contact the authors about a training correction notice** or use a historical resolved correction as new outreach. New first-contact clarification in an unrelated current paper requires the separate, already authorised, claim-specific high-evidence workflow and duplicate/Gmail checks. Do not mass-contact editors. Scientific or methodological disagreement is not a proven error.
8. **Storage & deduplication:** Keep training rows as public DOI links and short paraphrases; keep real manuscripts, private correspondence, research data and contact details out of the public repository. Workers save private receipts as already required; controller 09 is the only writer to the private master register. Coordinate by notice DOI, original DOI+claim/root ID, version and lane.
9. **Learning loop:** Workers propose an extra precise check or a synthetic unit test for a new error class. Controller 09 consolidates **non-duplicate** patterns and records whether a proposed rule was implemented, verified against a genuine case, rejected by a benign counterexample or deferred. Do not promise retraining or claim improved accuracy without prospective, held-out evaluation.

## Evaluation contract (before saying Synthesis improved)

- Split at **original article / trial-family level**, not individual claims; corrected articles used for rule design cannot be in held-out test data. Freeze source versions and tool/model/prompt variants.
- Compare same source-access budget and same inference conditions for original checklist vs notice-informed checklist.
- Report **counts** and denominators for: unique original DOIs, claims, true notice-supported corrections retrievable from the frozen original version, correctly localised hits, false positives, NO_FLAG, HOLD/NOT_COMPARABLE, time/reviewer burden and independent human adjudications.
- Distinguish **notice category matching** from actual **error discovery**, and do not call a software/prompt calibration exercise a measured prospective clinical validation.
- A published correction is a **historical label**, not a licence to infer other articles contain the same error. Record the strongest benign explanation and check it each time.
