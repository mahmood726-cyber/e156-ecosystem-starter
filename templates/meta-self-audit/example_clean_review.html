Prespecified analysis: random-effects pooling with the Hartung-Knapp (HKSJ) interval. The headline below is the HKSJ estimate.

Headline (HKSJ): risk ratio 0.82 (95% CI 0.71 to 0.95), k = 12 trials, 8,431 participants.

Harms

Serious adverse events were pooled as a co-primary outcome: risk ratio 1.04 (95% CI 0.88 to 1.23). All-cause discontinuation is reported separately as a tolerability proxy, not an adverse event.

Small-study effects

Egger's test was run because k = 12 exceeds the threshold of 10 recommended by the Cochrane Handbook: p = 0.41. The funnel plot is shown above.

Search

We searched MEDLINE, Embase and CENTRAL from 2010 to 2026. Last search run: 2026. Report generated: 2026. Eligibility was not restricted by publication date or by comparator type; both placebo-controlled and active-comparator trials were eligible.

PRISMA flow

Records identified: 1200. Duplicates removed: 200. Records screened: 1000. Records excluded: 940. Full-text articles assessed: 60. Full-text articles excluded: 48. Studies included: 12.

Registration

The protocol was committed before data extraction began. The commit timestamp is a tamper-evident local record of the protocol date. This review is not registered on PROSPERO.
